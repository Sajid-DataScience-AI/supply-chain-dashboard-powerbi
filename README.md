# 📊 Supply Chain & Logistics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project presents an **end-to-end Supply Chain & Logistics dashboard** built using Power BI to analyze business performance across Supplier, Inventory, Shipment, and Customer domains.  

The dasboard provides actionable insights into key KPIs such as revenue, profit, margin, delivery performance, inventory turnover, and shipment delays. It enables stakeholders to identify operational inefficiencies, monitor supply chain performance, and make data-driven business decision.

Key highlights include delay analysis by carrier, inventory optimization insights, supplier performance evaluation, and customer revenue trends.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall business performance using key KPIs  
- Identify operational inefficiencies in supply chain processes  
- Track shipment delays and delivery performance  
- Evaluate inventory health and defect trends  
- Understand customer behavior and profitability  

---

## 📌 Key Features
- Interactive dashboards with **slicers, drill-down, and navigation buttons**  
- KPI tracking: **Revenue, Profit, Profit Margin %, Delivery %, Inventory Metrics**  
- **Dynamic insights section** highlighting key business findings  
- Advanced visualizations including:
  - Trend analysis (monthly performance)
  - Scatter plots (discount vs revenue vs profit)
  - Comparative analysis (supplier, carrier, customer)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 🟢 Overview
- High-level summary of business performance  
- Key KPIs and quick insights  
- Navigation to detailed analysis pages  

### 🟡 Supplier Analysis
- Supplier performance based on **cost, lead time, and order quantity**  
- Country-wise lead time comparison  
- Identification of high-cost and high-delay suppliers  

### 🔵 Inventory & Production
- Inventory value trends and stock monitoring  
- Defect analysis by product  
- Stock vs Safety Stock vs Reorder Point comparison  

### 🟣 Shipment & Logistics
- Carrier performance (delay vs delivery)  
- Shipment cost trends  
- Delay reasons analysis  
- Shipment status distribution  

### 🟠 Customer & Sales
- Revenue, Profit, and Growth trends  
- Customer-wise performance  
- Discount impact on revenue and profitability  
- Category-wise profit margin analysis  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- High delays are concentrated in specific carriers, indicating optimization opportunities  
- Inventory peaks in certain months, suggesting demand-supply imbalance  
- High discounts in some categories reduce profitability  
- A few customers contribute the majority of revenue
